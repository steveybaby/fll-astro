# Practice Tournament — Innovation Project

Judged practice presentation at Piedmont Middle School, 740 Magnolia Ave, Piedmont.

## The day

- **10:00–10:15:** Team check-in at the desk outside Buzz Redford Gym, at the "top of the stairs" on Magnolia Ave.
- **10:15–10:25:** Coaches' meeting.
- **10:30–12:00:** Presentations. Each team gets one 30-minute slot covering both the innovation project and the robot design. Our exact slot wasn't in the email.

Coaches can sit in silently and record, but parents wait outside. There's no parking lot, so plan to walk from nearby streets and leave Moraga around 9:20.

## Inside the 30-minute slot (FIRST's official format this season)

1. Welcome and setup: 2 min
2. Innovation project presentation: 5 min. Judges can stop the team at 5 minutes, so rehearse to about 4:30.
3. Innovation project questions: 5 min
4. Robot design explanation: 5 min
5. Robot design questions: 5 min
6. Final share and feedback: up to 8 min

## Project questions the judges will ask

- What problem you chose and how it connects to the season prompt
- Your research sources
- How the work was split up
- What's innovative about your solution
- Who gave you feedback and what you changed
- How it helps others
